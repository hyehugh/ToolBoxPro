## Why Merge PDFs?

You have three separate PDFs — a cover page, a report, and an appendix — but you need one file to email or upload. Merging them should be instant and free.

Most PDF tools limit you to 2-3 merges per day or add watermarks. Not here.

## Why Would You Need to Merge PDFs?

PDF merging solves a common problem: scattered documents that belong together. Here are the most frequent scenarios:

### Combining Scanned Documents

If you scan a multi-page contract with a flatbed scanner, each page comes out as a separate PDF. Merging them turns those individual scans into a single, coherent document that's easy to email, print, or archive.

### Merging Invoices and Receipts

Accountants and small business owners often receive invoices from different vendors. Instead of sending a client 15 separate PDF files, merge everything into one consolidated file. It looks professional and reduces confusion.

### Joining Reports and Presentations

A quarterly report might have a title page, an executive summary, detailed analytics, and an appendix — each created separately. Merging them creates a single, polished deliverable.

### Creating E-Books and Portfolios

Writers compile chapters, photographers gather portfolios, designers collect project sheets. A merged PDF is the standard format for sharing completed collections.

## Tips for a Smooth Merge

- **Name your files** before uploading — the order in your file manager is a helpful starting point
- **Check page counts** — if your final document seems too short, one file may have failed to upload
- **Use consistent orientation** — mixing portrait and landscape pages works but may look awkward in a reader
- **Merge in batches** for more than 10 files — merge the first 10, download, then merge the result with the next batch

## FAQ

**What's the maximum file size?** PDFs up to ~100MB are handled smoothly. For larger files, performance depends on your browser's memory.

**Can I merge more than 10 PDFs?** Yes — do it in batches. Merge the first 10, download the result, then upload that result with the next batch.

**Is my data safe?** Yes. PDF processing happens locally using pdf-lib WebAssembly. Your files never reach any server.

**Does it work on mobile?** Yes — the drag-and-drop interface works on touch devices.

**Will my hyperlinks and bookmarks be preserved?** Basic text content and images are preserved. Interactive elements like form fields and JavaScript actions may not carry over in all cases.

**Can I merge different file types (Word, images, etc.) into PDF?** Our tool merges PDF with PDF. To combine different file types, first convert each file to PDF using a free converter, then merge them here.

**What if the merged file is too large to email?** Try compressing the final PDF with an online PDF compressor before attaching it.
